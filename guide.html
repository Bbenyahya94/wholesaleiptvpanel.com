A practical guide for customers and new resellers. No unexplained jargon, no invented compatibility claims.

01

Choose a service before a duration

Start with the panel catalog. Read the description, compare the public customer price and open the dedicated page. Product names such as “4K” or “8K” are branding and do not guarantee that every source is delivered at that resolution.

Best first question

Which service is currently recommended for my country, main device and preferred subscription length?

02

Check the device and connection rules

Before activation, confirm your main device, application and number of simultaneous connections. Smart TV, Android, Fire TV, mobile and computer setups may use different applications.

  • Main device and operating system
  • Preferred app or player
  • Stable internet connection
  • Number of simultaneous users
  • Country or region
03

Send a complete request

Every Get Started button prepares the service name, duration and price. Add your device and country in WhatsApp so the team can answer without repeating basic questions.

04

Keep your access details private

Do not publish subscription credentials, playlist URLs or panel logins. Save them in a secure password manager and contact support if a device change requires assistance.

05

Reseller checklist

Ask for the current starter requirement, credit logic, user duration, renewal process, compatible devices and support workflow. Do not base a purchase on screenshots or old prices alone.
